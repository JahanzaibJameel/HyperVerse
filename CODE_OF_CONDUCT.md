# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- **Be respectful**: Treat others with dignity and respect
- **Be inclusive**: Welcome newcomers and diverse perspectives
- **Be constructive**: Provide helpful, actionable feedback
- **Be collaborative**: Work together toward common goals
- **Be patient**: Understand that everyone has different experience levels
- **Be empathetic**: Consider the impact of your words and actions

### Unacceptable Behavior
- **Harassment**: Any form of unwelcome conduct
- **Discrimination**: Treatment based on protected characteristics
- **Personal attacks**: Insults, demeaning language, or ad hominem attacks
- **Trolling**: Deliberately provocative or off-topic messages
- **Spam**: Unsolicited promotional content
- **Doxxing**: Sharing private information without consent
- **Threats**: Any form of intimidation or threats of violence

## Enforcement

### Reporting
If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to:
- **Email**: conduct@hyperverse.app
- **GitHub**: Create a private issue with the "conduct" label
- **Discord**: Message any community moderator

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.

### Consequences
Community leaders may take any action they deem appropriate, including:
- **Warning**: Formal or informal warnings
- **Removal**: Temporary or permanent removal from the community
- **Ban**: Permanent ban from all community spaces
- **Legal action**: Involvement of law enforcement when necessary

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all community spaces, including:
- **GitHub repositories**: Issues, pull requests, discussions
- **Discord server**: All channels and direct messages
- **Events**: Online and offline community events
- **Social media**: Official HyperVerse accounts
- **Email communications**: Community-related emails

This code of conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Community Guidelines

### For Contributors
1. **Welcome newcomers**: Help new contributors get started
2. **Provide constructive feedback**: Focus on the code, not the person
3. **Be patient with questions**: Everyone was a beginner once
4. **Acknowledge good work**: Recognize and appreciate contributions
5. **Follow the contribution guidelines**: Respect established processes

### For Maintainers
1. **Lead by example**: Demonstrate positive behavior
2. **Be responsive**: Address issues and pull requests promptly
3. **Be fair**: Apply guidelines consistently
4. **Be transparent**: Explain decisions and processes
5. **Mentor others**: Help contributors grow and improve

### For All Community Members
1. **Assume good intentions**: Give others the benefit of the doubt
2. **Stay on topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the project
3. **Use inclusive language**: Avoid assumptions about others
4. **Respect boundaries**: Honor personal and professional boundaries
5. **Learn and grow**: Be open to feedback and self-improvement

## Conflict Resolution

### Step 1: Direct Communication
If you have a conflict with another community member:
1. **Reach out privately**: Send a respectful, private message
2. **Be specific**: Clearly describe the issue and desired outcome
3. **Listen actively**: Hear their perspective without interruption
4. **Find common ground**: Look for mutually acceptable solutions

### Step 2: Mediation
If direct communication doesn't resolve the issue:
1. **Contact a mediator**: Reach out to a community leader
2. **Explain the situation**: Provide context and relevant details
3. **Participate in mediation**: Engage in good faith
4. **Accept the outcome**: Respect the mediator's decision

### Step 3: Formal Process
For serious or persistent issues:
1. **File a formal report**: Use the reporting channels above
2. **Provide evidence**: Share relevant documentation
3. **Cooperate with investigation**: Participate as needed
4. **Accept the resolution**: Honor the final decision

## Diversity and Inclusion

- **Accessibility improvements**: Make our project usable by everyone
